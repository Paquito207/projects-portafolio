Ocean Crown Shipping Service was founded in Dubai, the United Arab Emirates, with a vision to revolutionize international freight forwarding. Backed by professionals who have decades of global experience in the shipping industry, we provide international freight forwarding by sea, air, and land.

With the headquarters of Ocean Logistics located in Amman (Capital), Jordan, and our other branches located respectively in Kuwait, Iraq, Armenia, and Belgium, we ensure global reach with local expertise.
